      How items are rated

      Relevance

      Blocker
      Without it, Keycloak can't run lawfully or practically in a production EUDI deployment. Workarounds don't count if they don't scale, such as registering every wallet as a client or reissuing credentials every day. Also security holes that give access to other users' credentials.
      High
      Required by law or HAIP, or needed by most deployments. Also security issues that take more than one step to exploit.
      Medium
      Better EUDI flows, or needed by some deployments.
      Normal
      Needed work that EUDI flows don't wait for, such as admin UI, docs, tests and refactoring. Also formats that EUDI doesn't use.

      Basis

      Legal
      Required by an implementing regulation, or by an ARF or ETSI rule that a regulation refers to.
      Profile
      Required by HAIP or the OpenID4VC specs.
      Usable
      No rule requires it, but real deployments need it. For example, the German PID has no stable identifier, so relying parties also need a credential of their own.
      Security
      Fixes a weakness.
      Hygiene
      Cleanup, UI, docs or tests.

      Open items are rated by hand. Two more reviewers rated them again without seeing the authors. Where both disagreed with a rating, it was changed. New items are rated by Claude Code once a day. Finished items are rated by topic only.

        The data comes from the GitHub API. An item is included if it has the area/oid4vc label, belongs to an OpenID4VC epic, or names an OpenID4VC topic in its title or text.
